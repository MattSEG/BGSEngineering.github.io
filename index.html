Designation

Discontinuous Advanced Resin Composite. A quasi-isotropic matrix built from short discontinuous carbon fiber, engineered to process at scale without the autoclave constraint that defines conventional composites.

The difference

Conventional composites force a trade: performance or cost. DARC collapses that trade. Engineered to be net-shape from the mold, it eliminates the autoclave constraint and compresses the manufacturing timeline in ways that change the unit economics of high-performance structure.

The property set

Specific strength at titanium parity. EMI shielding and ESD capability engineered into the matrix. Environmental durability designed in, not added on. Patent pending.
