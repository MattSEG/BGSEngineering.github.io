STRENGTH Confirmed Property

DARC delivers structural performance on par with continuous-fiber composites — but with one critical difference: it doesn't shatter. The discontinuous fiber architecture arrests crack propagation through fiber pull-out friction rather than catastrophic fracture.

The result is pseudo-ductile failure — progressive energy absorption rather than brittle collapse. Airframes survive hard landings. Payloads survive crashes. Operators recover assets instead of losing them.

In combat drone operations, an airframe that fractures cleanly and predictably under overload is worth more than one that holds — then suddenly doesn't. Crack-arrest architecture provides a measurable, repeatable safety margin rather than a brittle cliff edge.

Void-free consolidation at 1.37 g/cm³ means no hidden porosity, no weak inclusions, no internal delamination paths. Dense, homogeneous composite — every time.

EMI

At 0.86 Ω bulk resistance, the continuous conductive fiber network inside the DARC airframe presents a massive impedance mismatch with free space. Radar energy reflects. High-power microwave pulses — the same ones designed to fry drone electronics — hit an outer skin that looks like a conductor and turn back.

Waves that do penetrate encounter multiple internal reflections and dielectric loss from the conductive matrix, converting microwave energy to low-grade heat volumetrically rather than letting it reach avionics.

DAMPING Highly Likely

The discontinuous fiber architecture provides superior mechanical damping compared to continuous-fiber laminates. Millions of chip-to-chip fiber interfaces absorb vibration under dynamic loading — converting rotor harmonics and airframe resonance into low-grade heat rather than transmitting them to payload optics and sensors.

For gimbaled EO/IR payloads and synthetic aperture radar, the mount is the isolator. Precision optical systems that normally require active stabilization benefit from passive attenuation built into the structure.

For sensor mounts specifically: the damping is intrinsic. No elastomeric bushings. No isolation platforms. No added mass.

UV

The resin system in DARC is engineered from the ground up for outdoor permanence. The matrix backbone is inherently UV-stable at the molecular level — it doesn't undergo the chain scission and chalking that degrades standard epoxies. Multilayer UV protection handles the surface and subsurface.

The result: no chalking, no yellowing, no surface crazing — under continuous outdoor exposure. No protective coatings required. No recoating intervals.

FOUL

The resin surface migrates during cure to form a superhydrophobic, low-energy boundary layer. Marine biological contaminants — barnacles, algae, biofilm — cannot anchor effectively to this surface.

For maritime patrol drones, ship-launched UAVs, and littoral UAS platforms, this eliminates the fouling problem entirely — without biocide coatings, without scheduled hull cleaning, without performance degradation over deployment duration.

This surface is not applied. It is the surface. It cannot be worn off.
