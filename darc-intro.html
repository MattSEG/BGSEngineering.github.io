What we know

Shown in filled type

Strong
A structural material that carries real load.
Light
Lighter than aluminum.
Moldable
Chopped carbon fiber, pressed into the shape of the part.
Complex geometry
Ribs, bosses, curves and thickness changes in one molding.
Integrated parts
Plates and fasteners become one piece. Threaded inserts are molded in.
Conductive
Conductive through the material itself, with no added mesh, foil or coating.
EMI shielding
Early RF testing shows it shields. The full extent is being measured now.
UV durability
Built in by design, from the resin up, with national laboratory advisors.

What we are testing

Shown in outlined type

Radar signature reduction
A hypothesis the physics supports. Characterization will answer it either way.
Structural health monitoring
A conductive structure may be able to sense its own strain and damage. Under investigation.
